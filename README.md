# Student Profile Page
## 2602727-lab3

## Project description

This project is a student profile webpage created for the School of Computing & IT Week of Welcome. It introduces me, Anthony Granston, as a first-year BSc Computing student majoring in Computer Science. I designed the page to give visitors a brief introduction to me, my studies, and where they can find me during orientation.

## What the webpage includes

The page opens with a welcome section and navigation menu. The About Me section shares a short biography, my programme and year of study, my interests, and a photograph. A Week of Welcome feature tells visitors where they can meet me on campus.

The Courses section presents five courses I am studying, with a short description of each. The Weekly Schedule section shows the days and times of my classes. The Contact section provides a form where visitors can enter their name, email address, and a message. The footer identifies me and the University of Technology, Jamaica.

## Design and technologies

I used HTML5 to organize the page content and CSS3 to control its appearance. The markup includes semantic elements such as `header`, `nav`, `main`, `section`, `article`, `aside`, and `footer`, along with headings, paragraphs, links, images, tables, and form controls. I also used `div` and `span` elements in the layout.

I separated the page content from its presentation by placing the main styles in `style.css` and the tablet and mobile media queries in `responsive.css`. The responsive styles adjust the navigation and page layouts for smaller screens. The project uses no JavaScript, Bootstrap, or other front-end frameworks.
